SECTION A — PLOT SIZE CATEGORIES (DEMO LOGIC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Small plot: under 600 sq.ft — tighter setbacks and height limits often apply.
Medium plot: 600 to 2400 sq.ft — typical individual house range in urban demos.
Large plot: above 2400 sq.ft — may allow more footprint flexibility; still FSI-bound.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION B — FSI (FLOOR SPACE INDEX) — RESIDENTIAL DEMO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Floor Space Index is the ratio of total permissible built-up area to plot area.
Demo residential assumption for Chennai-style narrative: FSI = 1.5.
Formula (demo): max built-up ≈ plot area × FSI.
Example: plot 1200 sq.ft × 1.5 = 1800 sq.ft maximum built-up (all floors combined),
subject to setbacks, height, road width, and authority rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION C — SETBACKS (APPROXIMATE DEMO, MEDIUM PLOTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For plots roughly 1000–1500 sq.ft in dense urban context (illustrative):
Front setback toward main road: about 1.5 m.
Rear setback: about 1.5 m.
Side setbacks: about 1 m each.
Purposes: light, ventilation, fire access, privacy.
Corner plots: extra setback may be required on additional road-facing sides.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION D — HEIGHT & NUMBER OF FLOORS (DEMO HEURISTIC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Allowed height and floors depend on: road width, plot size, FSI, zone, and approval.
Demo example: 1200 sq.ft plot with 30 ft road — narrative often allows G+2
(Ground plus two upper floors) if built-up fits within FSI after setbacks.
Taller buildings need stair/lift/fire rules per NBC and local codes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION E — PARKING (DEMO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Individual houses above ~100 sq.m often need at least one car parking space.
Typical minimum stall size (order of magnitude): 2.5 m × 5 m.
Apartments: commonly one parking space per dwelling unit unless authority specifies
differently for small units.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION F — MINIMUM ROOM SIZES (PRACTICE GUIDE, NOT A LEGAL SUBSTITUTE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Living room: about 10 ft × 12 ft minimum comfortable span for small homes.
Bedroom: about 10 ft × 10 ft minimum for one bed and wardrobe.
Kitchen: about 8 ft × 6 ft workable galley or L-shape start.
Bathroom: about 4 ft × 7 ft for WC + shower in compact plans.
Staircase: about 3 ft clear width minimum for residential comfort (verify code).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION G — SPACE PLANNING ZONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Public zone: living, entry, sometimes dining visible from entrance.
Private zone: bedrooms, family areas away from main door line of sight.
Service zone: kitchen, utility, toilets; keep toilet doors not facing dining directly
when possible. Cross-ventilation preferred for living and bedrooms.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION H — BHK SHORTHAND (USER-FRIENDLY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1 BHK: one bedroom, hall, kitchen — compact urban apartments.
2 BHK: two bedrooms — common family format.
3 BHK: three bedrooms — growing family or work-from-home room.
4 BHK: four bedrooms — luxury or multi-generational; check carpet vs saleable area.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION J — FAQ (RETRIEVAL-FRIENDLY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What is FSI?
A: Ratio of permissible built-up floor area to plot area; higher FSI allows more
   floor plate if setbacks and height allow.

Q: What is setback?
A: Mandatory open strip between building line and site boundary for safety and light.

Q: Can I build three full floors on 1200 sq.ft in Chennai?
A: Often discussed as G+2; exact approval depends on road width, zone, and current
   development control rules — confirm with CMDA/consultant.

Q: Is parking mandatory?
A: Usually yes for car-owning households in plotted and many apartment norms.

Q: What about earthquake or wind?
A: NBC and IS codes govern structural design; a structural engineer must design and
   certify — not inferred from chat alone.
