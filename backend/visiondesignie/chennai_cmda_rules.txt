VISIONDESIGNIE — CHENNAI / CMDA (HIGH-LEVEL RAG, DEMO)
Disclaimer: Official control rules change by notification and zone. Always verify with CMDA and your architect.

CMDA ROLE
- Development control for Chennai metro limits: setbacks, height, coverage, parking, land use.
- Reviews building plans against applicable regulations for that pocket and road class.

BUILDING APPROVAL (WHAT HOMEOWNERS HEAR)
- Application with site dimensions, block plans, floor plans, sections, structural stability route when triggered.
- Queries on setback lines, parking count, height — answered with drawing revisions.

BASIC COMPLIANCE IDEAS
- Setbacks from site boundaries as per schedule for plot width / road class.
- Maximum height and floors tied to road width, plot size, and zone — not one number for all Chennai.
- Parking and access for emergency vehicles on larger buildings.

PLAN APPROVAL IMPORTANCE
- Sanctioned plan is the legal reference for what may be built; deviations risk notice or future sale issues.
- Mortgage and insurance sometimes ask for approved drawings.

CHATBOT PHRASE
- “CMDA checks your drawing against the rule book for your street class — your consultant gives exact metres.”
