VISIONDESIGNIE — TAMIL NADU MATERIALS AND SITE PRACTICE (RAG)

COMMON MATERIALS
- RCC frame with table-moulded brick or AAC block infill — widely used in cities.
- Vitrified tiles for floors; ceramic anti-skid in wet areas.
- MS fabrication for gates and grills; SS or powder coat for coastal corrosion resistance near sea breeze belts.

BRICK VS CONCRETE TALK
- Traditional fired brick still common; AAC popular for speed and lighter dead load when engineer agrees.
- Stone aggregate quality and curing discipline matter more than brand noise on small sites.

COST REALISM (STATE MOOD)
- Steel and cement cycles move monthly — budget contingency standard on Tamil Nadu sites.
- Saving on waterproofing hurts within two monsoons — local contractors admit this quietly.

LOCAL TRENDS
- UPVC windows common; textured paint + selective cladding for elevation.
- Rooftop solar water heater and small PV arrays increasing in middle-income builds.

CONSULTANT PHRASE
- “Spend on structure shell and water tightness first; finishes can phase after house is safe and dry.”
