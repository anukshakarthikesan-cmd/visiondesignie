ENTRANCE (COMMON PREFERENCE TALK)

KITCHEN (COMMON PREFERENCE TALK)

- Still need: chimney, cross breeze, gas cylinder / pipeline safety, counter workflow.

MASTER BEDROOM (COMMON PREFERENCE TALK)

CHILDREN / GUEST ROOM

TOILETS

PUJA ROOM
