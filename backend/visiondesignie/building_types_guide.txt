VISIONDESIGNIE — BUILDING TYPES (INDIA, RAG)

RESIDENTIAL — 1 BHK
- One bedroom, hall, kitchen, bath — compact urban format.
- Needs: clear entry, minimal passage, one good daylight wall for living.

RESIDENTIAL — 2 BHK
- Two bedrooms, hall, kitchen, usually 1–2 baths — common family flat.
- Needs: storage, sometimes study corner; parking per norms.

RESIDENTIAL — 3 BHK
- Three bedrooms — growing family or work-from-home room.
- Needs: utility, maybe maid bath separate; watch saleable vs carpet area in ads.

APARTMENTS
- Shared walls, common stairs/lift, fire rules scale with height and occupancy.
- Needs: service shaft, meter room, refuge/fire rules for tall towers.

VILLAS
- Low-rise individual home on plot — more façade freedom; setbacks still apply.
- Needs: landscape, boundary wall, gate sweep, outdoor services.

COMMERCIAL — SHOP / OFFICE
- Customer path, display, staff area, toilet, storage; parking by use and floor area.

COMMERCIAL — MALL / SUPERMARKET
- Core placement, escalators, fire exits, loading dock — specialist planning.

SCHOOLS (BASICS)
- Classrooms with light and ventilation, assembly exits, playground access, labs with safety.

HOSPITALS (BASICS)
- Zoning: OPD, IPD, diagnostics, infection control — not residential rule set.

CHATBOT USE
- Match vocabulary to type — don’t apply house setback chat to mall fire exits without context.
