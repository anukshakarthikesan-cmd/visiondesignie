VISIONDESIGNIE — DESIGN GUIDANCE (THEMES, MATERIALS, ELEVATION) RAG

THEMES (CONVERSATION STARTERS)
- “Do you lean modern clean lines, or traditional roof and mouldings?”
- “Warm palette uses wood tones and off-white; cool palette uses grey and glass accents.”
- “Minimal means fewer grooves; classic means cornices and defined windows.”

MATERIALS — FACADE
- “Textured paint is economical; stone cladding is selective — best on one feature wall.”
- “Brick jaali gives shade and pattern; it needs cleaning access.”
- “UPVC windows are common; wood look needs maintenance in humid coast cities.”

MATERIALS — INTERIOR
- “Flooring: vitrified tiles are durable; wood finish tiles give warmth without full wood cost.”
- “Kitchen: quartz or granite counters; backsplash tile for easy wipe.”
- “Ceiling: simple flat with cove light is calm; false ceiling drops height — use where slab is high.”

INTERIOR IDEAS
- “Keep circulation straight — fewer corners make furniture placement easy.”
- “Built-in wardrobes save 6–8 inches versus loose furniture in tight rooms.”
- “Study nook by a window is pleasant if sun is not harsh.”

ELEVATION IDEAS
- “Rhythm wins: repeat window widths; one strong vertical or horizontal band ties the face.”
- “Parapet height and railing detail change the whole silhouette.”
- “Canopy over entry is practical rain cover and a visual anchor.”

LIGHTING MOOD
- “Warm 3000K for living and bedrooms; slightly cooler for kitchen task areas.”

SUSTAINABILITY NUDGES
- “Rainwater recharge and shaded west wall are low-cost comfort moves.”

WHEN USER HAS NO OPINION
- “I suggest a calm modern base: light façade, darker accents only at porch and sill.”
