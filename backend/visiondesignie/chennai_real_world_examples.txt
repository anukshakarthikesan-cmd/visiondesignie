VISIONDESIGNIE — CHENNAI SAMPLE CASES (DEMO / TEACHING, RAG)
Illustrative only — not approval advice.

CASE 1
Input: Plot 1200 sq.ft, Chennai, road 30 ft, own residence.
Output-style summary:
- Floors often discussed: G+2 in classroom examples — confirm with authority.
- FSI talk: demo 1.5 → ~1800 sq.ft total built-up concept before setbacks trim footprint.
- Setbacks (teaching band): front ~1.5 m, rear ~1.5 m, sides ~1 m — verify schedule.
- Parking: at least one car bay typical expectation.
- Layout hint: ground living + kitchen + one bed; upper two beds; top terrace / utility.

CASE 2
Input: Plot 800 sq.ft, narrow frontage, Velachery-type dense lane.
Output-style:
- Stair on side to save width; single car tight or scooter-first realistic.
- Height may be more limited if road class is lower — check table.
- Cross-ventilation harder — plan high window or light well early.

CASE 3
Input: 2400 sq.ft corner, two roads, Tambaram belt.
Output-style:
- Choose main entry for quiet vs prestige; corner visibility splay may apply.
- Parking two cars more feasible; garden or portico eats footprint — balance FSI.
- G+2 still common discussion; watch lift trigger heights in rules if applicable.

CASE 4
Input: Apartment 1100 sq.ft saleable, OMR, 3 BHK ask.
Output-style:
- Ask carpet vs saleable; study nook vs third tiny bedroom trade-off.
- West sun balcony — suggest deep slab or sliding shutters.

CASE 5
Input: Ground shop + first floor residence, T. Nagar edge.
Output-style:
- Split circulation customer vs family stair; parking for shop peak hours tough — realistic brief.
- Fire and assembly loads higher — insist architect specialist path.
