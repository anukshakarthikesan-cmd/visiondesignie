VISIONDESIGNIE — CORE ARCHITECTURE DATASET (CHENNAI / TAMIL NADU DEMO)
Use as teaching-style context. Final numbers always come from survey + local authority + architect.

SECTION: FSI AND BUILT-UP (RESIDENTIAL, DEMO)
- FSI (Floor Space Index): ratio of total permissible built-up floor area to plot area.
- Typical classroom value for Chennai-style residential chat: 1.5 (illustrative only).
- Example: plot 1200 sq.ft × 1.5 ≈ 1800 sq.ft total built-up across all floors if rules allow.
- Setbacks and height still limit footprint — you cannot always use full FSI on every floor plate.
- Built-up is not the same as saleable or carpet area in apartment ads.

SECTION: SETBACKS (APPROXIMATE DEMO, MEDIUM PLOTS)
- For plots roughly 1000–1500 sq.ft (teaching band): front ~1.5 m, rear ~1.5 m, sides ~1 m each.
- Purpose: light, ventilation, fire access, privacy, sometimes future road reserve.
- Corner plots: extra margin or splay on second road front — confirm on site plan.
- Measure from legal boundary in survey, not from neighbour’s compound guess.

SECTION: ROAD WIDTH AND HEIGHT / FLOORS
- Wider front roads often support better access and more height in authority tables.
- Example often used in demos: 1200 sq.ft + 30 ft road → discussion of G+2 (Ground + 2 upper).
- Narrow lanes may cap storeys even when FSI math looks generous.
- Lift, refuge, and fire rules increase for taller apartments — not the same as small houses.

SECTION: PARKING
- Many norms expect at least one car space for individual houses above common size thresholds.
- Typical planning bay size order: ~2.5 m × 5 m (starting point before detailed layout).
- Apartments: often one slot per unit in planning talk; visitor parking varies by project class.
- Stilt parking: counts in massing and access design — slope and turning at gate matter.

SECTION: ROOM SIZE GUIDES (COMFORT, NOT LEGAL SUBSTITUTE)
- Living: about 10 × 12 ft minimum comfortable for small Indian homes.
- Bedroom: about 10 × 10 ft for one bed + wardrobe start point.
- Kitchen: about 8 × 6 ft workable galley or L-shape.
- Bathroom: about 4 × 7 ft for compact WC + shower.
- Stair: about 3 ft clear width common residential target; uniform riser height for safety.

SECTION: ZONING AND FLOW
- Public zone: living, entry, guest path.
- Private zone: bedrooms, family areas away from main door line.
- Service zone: kitchen, utility, toilets — short paths, easy maintenance.
- Kitchen near dining; common toilet reachable without walking through bedrooms.
- Cross-ventilation when two opposite sides open; Chennai heat favours shade on harsh west.

SECTION: BHK SHORTHAND
- 1 BHK: one bedroom, hall, kitchen — compact flats.
- 2 BHK: two bedrooms — common family format.
- 3 BHK: third room for child, guest, or study — check carpet vs saleable in brochures.

SECTION: CMDA AND APPROVALS (HIGH LEVEL)
- CMDA: development control for Chennai metro limits — setbacks, height, parking, use.
- Process: measured site + drawings + structural path when required → submission → queries → sanction.
- Build to match sanctioned plan; changes need revision drawings.

SECTION: NBC (BIG PICTURE)
- National Building Code: minimum thinking for fire, life safety, accessibility, services.
- Local body applies NBC through checklists; tall and crowded buildings face stricter rules.

SECTION: STRUCTURE AND SAFETY
- RCC frame with brick or AAC infill is typical urban TN practice.
- Structural design and soil must be signed off by qualified engineer — not guessed from chat.

SECTION: CHENNAI CLIMATE
- Hot and humid long season: favour cross-ventilation, roof insulation, west sun control.
- Monsoon: terrace waterproofing, balcony drips, ground plinth and site drainage matter.

SECTION: VASTU (CONVERSATION LAYER, TN)
- Many clients ask southeast kitchen, southwest master, northeast pooja — blend with light and structure.
- Treat as family preference unless they bring a dedicated vastu advisor.

SECTION: MATERIALS (PRACTICAL)
- Vitrified floors, anti-skid wet areas, UPVC windows common; good waterproofing pays back fast.

SECTION: DEMO CASE (FULL)

Input:
Plot: 1200 sq.ft
Location: Chennai
Road width: 30 ft
Use: Own residence

Output-style summary:
- Floors (demo talk): G+2 often discussed — verify with CMDA schedule.
- FSI (demo): 1.5 → ~1800 sq.ft total built-up concept before setbacks trim footprint.
- Setbacks (demo band): front/rear ~1.5 m, sides ~1 m — verify survey.
- Parking: at least one car typical expectation.
- Suggested layout (text):
  Ground: living, kitchen, one bedroom, common bath.
  First: two bedrooms, bath, small circulation.
  Second: terrace / utility / optional small room if within FSI and height.

SECTION: MORE FAQ

Q: What is FSI?
A: It scales how much total floor area you may build relative to plot size; setbacks and height still apply.

Q: What is setback?
A: Mandatory open strip inside your boundary before the building line — for safety and light.

Q: Can I build three full floors?
A: Often discussed as G+2 for houses; exact approval depends on road class, zone, and current notification.

Q: What is carpet area?
A: Roughly usable floor inside walls; saleable area in ads may add common walls and amenities.

Q: Do I need an architect on record?
A: For sanctioned drawings and many submissions, yes — Council of Architecture governs use of title architect in India.

Q: Is parking mandatory?
A: Often yes for car-owning households in plotted housing and many apartment norms — confirm locally.

SECTION: ARCHITECT CHATBOT BEHAVIOUR
- Ask plot size, location, road width, family size, bedroom count before locking a plan.
- Give structured bullets and options; explain trade-offs in plain English.
- Say when numbers are illustrative; send users to licensed architect + authority for binding compliance.
