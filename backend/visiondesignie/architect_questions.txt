VISIONDESIGNIE — NATURAL QUESTIONS AN ARCHITECT ASKS (RAG)

SITE & CONTEXT
- “May I have your plot area, and if you know it, the approximate length and width?”
- “Which city or local body area is this — corporation, municipality, or village panchayat?”
- “Is the plot on one road or a corner? Any side open space?”
- “What is the road width in front — in feet or meters?”
- “North direction: do you know which side is road-facing?”

USE & FAMILY
- “Who will use this building — only family, tenants, or home office too?”
- “How many bedrooms do you need as a minimum?”
- “Any elderly person or wheelchair use to plan for?”
- “Do you need a puja room, study, or work-from-home nook?”

MASSING & FLOORS
- “How many floors do you hope for — ground only, G+1, G+2?”
- “Do you want a terrace you can use, or mostly roof for services?”
- “Basement or stilt parking — is that on your mind?”

SERVICES & PARKING
- “How many cars and two-wheelers should we plan for?”
- “Do you prefer kitchen open to dining, or separate?”
- “Solar water heater or rooftop solar — interested?”

STYLE & BUDGET (SOFT, NOT JUDGMENTAL)
- “In three words, how should the home feel — bright, cozy, grand?”
- “Rough budget band helps me suggest materials — low, medium, or flexible?”

COMPLIANCE AWARENESS
- “Do you already have an architect, or are you still in early ideas?”
- “Any notice from authority we should respect — setback line, lake buffer, etc.?”

CHECK-IN QUESTIONS (KEEP CLARITY)
- “Did I capture that correctly?”
- “What is most important to you — large kitchen, extra bedroom, or front look?”
- “Is there anything you definitely do not want?”
