VISIONDESIGNIE — MATERIALS AND CONSTRUCTION (BASICS, RAG)

STRUCTURE (COMMON)
- Reinforced concrete frame: columns, beams, slab — most common urban housing.
- Steel: industrial spans, some modern homes — needs anti-rust care.
- Load-bearing brick: older/low-rise contexts — openings limited by wall length.

WALLS
- Brick / AAC blocks: AAC lighter, needs correct fixing for shelves and grabs.
- External: weather barrier + crack control; internal: smooth finish for paint.

FLOORING
- Vitrified tiles: hard-wearing, common living/bedrooms.
- Ceramic: baths and balconies — anti-skid where wet.
- Wood-look tile: warm look, easier maintenance than solid wood in humid cities.

WALL FINISHES
- Acrylic paint economical; textured paint hides minor unevenness.
- Stone cladding: feature walls — plan support and seepage details at joints.

ROOF TYPES
- RCC slab with weathering course / tile on top — typical flat roof.
- Pitched tile roof: faster rain shed in heavy rain regions; attic or ceiling space varies.

DURABILITY BASICS
- Good waterproofing at terrace, bath, and window sills saves long-term repair.
- Cheap plumbing fixtures often cost more in leaks — balance spec with warranty.

COST BASICS (HIGH LEVEL)
- Structure and waterproofing are poor places to over-cut; finishes can phase later.
