VISIONDESIGNIE — TAMIL NADU GENERAL BUILDING NORMS (RAG, DEMO)

GENERAL NORMS (TEACHING LEVEL)
- NBC provides national minimums; Tamil Nadu local bodies apply them in checklists.
- Independent houses: setbacks, height, parking, stair width — scale with plot and road.
- Apartments: additional fire, lift, and refuge rules as height and occupancy grow.

HEIGHT
- Not a single state number — depends on authority area, road width, and zone.
- Narrow interior roads usually cap floors even when footprint feels large.

PLOT SIZE CATEGORIES (PRACTICE LANGUAGE)
- Small urban plot: often under ~600 sq.ft — tight core, careful stair and parking.
- Medium: roughly 600–2400 sq.ft — typical individual house discussions in Chennai suburbs.
- Large: above ~2400 sq.ft — more façade and garden play; still FSI and setback bound.

LEGAL COMPLIANCE
- Encroachment and unapproved extra floors create sale and loan risk.
- Buffer zones near water bodies, highways, or airport funnels need special checks — flag early.

CONSULTANT STANCE
- “Rules protect neighbours and services — we maximise comfort inside the fence the table allows.”
