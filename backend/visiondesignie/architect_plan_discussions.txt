VISIONDESIGNIE — PLAN DISCUSSION PHRASES (RAG)

OPENING A PLAN TALK
- “Let’s walk the plan as if you enter from the road.”
- “I’ll start with ground floor; we add upper floors only after this feels right.”

ZONING & PRIVACY
- “We keep bedrooms in a quieter zone, away from the main door.”
- “Living and dining take the front where guests arrive — that keeps private life calm.”
- “Service areas — kitchen, utility, toilets — sit best with short paths, not long hikes.”

LIGHT & AIR
- “Based on your plot size, I suggest the living room here for better cross ventilation.”
- “Staircase placement matters: mid-plan saves length; side placement frees a full room.”
- “If this side is hotter in summer, we shift sleeping rooms to the cooler side when possible.”

ROOM RELATIONSHIPS
- “Kitchen near dining saves daily steps; that’s comfort, not luxury.”
- “Common toilet should be reachable from living without walking through bedrooms.”
- “Master bedroom with attached bath works if size allows; else we keep one good shared bath.”

SIZE DISCIPLINE
- “On your area, a compact kitchen still works if the counter layout is straight or L-shaped.”
- “If you want a third bedroom, we may shrink passage width slightly — still within comfort.”

OPTIONS
- “Option A: larger living, smaller third bedroom.”
- “Option B: smaller living, all bedrooms more equal.”

CORNER PLOTS
- “Corner plots get two fronts — we decide which is ‘main’ for entry and height rules.”

APPROVAL FRAMING
- “This is a planning direction. Your architect will draw exact lines for sanction.”

USER UNCERTAINTY
- “If you are unsure, we start with a simple box plan and refine after one night’s think.”
