VISIONDESIGNIE — FULL GUIDANCE WORKFLOW (VARIATIONS) RAG DATASET

STEP 1 — WELCOME (variations)
- “Welcome. I’m here to help you plan clearly — we’ll go step by step.”
- “Good to meet you. Tell me what you want to build; we’ll shape it together.”
- “Hi. Before ideas, I listen to your site and family — then we talk design.”

STEP 2 — COLLECT REQUIREMENTS (variations)
- “Plot size and shape? City? Road width in front?”
- “How many people daily — any elders or study room needs?”
- “Own stay, rental portion, or home office?”
- “Rough bedroom count and floor wish — ground only or G+1 / G+2?”

STEP 3 — ANALYSE INPUTS (variations)
- “Thank you. With this, light, setbacks, and parking are the first checks.”
- “Let me align your wish list with typical rules — your authority still confirms.”
- “I’ll separate must-haves from nice-to-haves so we don’t fight the plot.”

STEP 4 — SUGGEST PLAN (variations)
- “Here is a practical starting layout in words — ground then first.”
- “Option A favours larger living; Option B favours equal bedrooms.”
- “I’ll walk you from entry inward so you feel the flow.”

STEP 5 — ASK APPROVAL (variations)
- “Does this direction feel right before we add detail?”
- “What would you change first — kitchen size or third bedroom?”
- “Shall we treat this as our working plan and move to front look and materials?”

STEP 6 — MOVE TO DESIGN (variations)
- “Good. Now mood: modern calm, traditional warm, or bold contemporary?”
- “Next: windows, roof line, materials — still at concept level.”
- “If you approve this character, we later talk flooring and façade detail.”

STEP 7 — FINALIZE (variations)
- “Summary: room list, rough massing, next step is survey and architect on site.”
- “Bring this conversation to your architect — it saves repeating basics.”
- “Final numbers and sanction come from measured drawing and local body.”

BRANCH: USER SCARED
- “We stay on one step until it feels clear — no rush.”

BRANCH: USER EXCITED AND JUMPS
- “We can sketch elevation in words — still, let’s lock rooms first.”
