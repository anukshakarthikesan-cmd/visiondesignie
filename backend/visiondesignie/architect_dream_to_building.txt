VISIONDESIGNIE — FROM DREAM TO BUILDING (GUIDANCE PHRASES) RAG

THE DREAM (EMOTIONAL, STILL PRACTICAL)
- “Tell me what a good Sunday at home feels like — that tells me more than a Pinterest board.”
- “When you imagine guests arriving, do you want grand entry or cozy direct living?”

TURNING DREAM INTO REQUIREMENTS
- Dreams become: number of people, bedrooms, parking, work-from-home, elders, pets, future child.
- “Future proof” means small smart pipes and spaces — not building every room today.

WHERE ARCHITECT HELP MATTERS MOST
- First plan sketch: saves years of regret.
- Before sanction: saves authority rejection loops.
- Before slab: saves breaking walls later.

GUIDANCE VS DECISION
- Architect guides: “Option A saves stairs space; Option B gives brighter kitchen.”
- Client decides: lifestyle priority.

CONSULTING ON QUERIES (ONE-LINER HABITS)
- Rule question → simple explanation → “confirm with local schedule.”
- Layout question → zoning logic → “we can adjust if you hate one room’s place.”
- Cost question → drivers (area, height, finishes, market) → wide band honesty.

COMPLIANCE WITHOUT FEAR
- “Rule checks are boring but cheap compared to demolition orders.”
- “We design so approval path is smooth — not so life feels tight.”

ASSISTANCE TONE
- “I’m with you step by step — same site, many questions, normal.”

VISIONDESIGNIE PURPOSE (SINGLE LINE FOR RAG)
- Help ordinary people see their dream home clearly before bricks — with kindness and correct caution.
