VISIONDESIGNIE — CHUNK PACK FOR VECTOR SEARCH (ONE IDEA PER BLOCK)
Use each === block as a separate embedding chunk for FAISS-style retrieval.

===CHUNK===
Topic: FSI definition. Floor Space Index relates total allowed built-up area to
plot area. If FSI is 1.5 and plot is 1200 sq.ft, demo max built-up is about 1800 sq.ft.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Setback front. For medium urban plots around 1000–1500 sq.ft, a demo front
setback of about 1.5 m toward the main road is often used in training examples.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Setback rear. Rear setback near 1.5 m helps light and services access in
compact residential layouts.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Setback side. Side setbacks near 1 m each reduce mutual overlooking and help
fire ladder access in theory — exact meters come from local schedules.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Floors and road width. Wider roads generally support more height and access;
a 30 ft road with 1200 sq.ft plot is a common student example for G+2 discussion.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Parking stall size. A typical car parking bay is often sized around 2.5 m by
5 m in preliminary planning before detailed parking layout.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Living room size. Minimum comfortable living room for small Indian homes
often starts near 10 ft by 12 ft before furniture.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Bedroom size. A compact bedroom often starts near 10 ft by 10 ft for one bed
and wardrobe in middle-income housing.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Kitchen adjacency. Kitchen works best adjacent to dining with short service
path; avoid placing kitchen door directly opposite toilet door when possible.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Privacy zoning. Bedrooms should sit in a quieter zone away from the main
entrance; living and dining handle circulation and guests.

===CHUNK===
Topic: 2 BHK meaning. Two bedrooms plus hall and kitchen — common family flat type
in Indian cities; check super area vs carpet area when buying.

===CHUNK===
Topic: 3 BHK meaning. Three bedrooms plus hall and kitchen — popular for families
with children or home office.

===CHUNK===
Topic: NBC role. National Building Code of India sets minimum standards for fire,
life safety, accessibility, and structural coordination — local bodies adopt it.

===CHUNK===
Topic: CMDA role. Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority governs building plan
approval in its jurisdiction; rules change by notification.

===CHUNK===
Topic: COA role. Council of Architecture regulates use of title “architect” in India;
only registered architects may sign certain statutory documents.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Corner plot. Extra road frontage may require additional setback or design
controls on the secondary frontage.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Terrace use. Roof terraces are popular for utility, solar, and water tanks;
structural load and parapet height must be engineered.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Vastu conversation. Many clients ask for kitchen southeast or master bedroom
southwest; treat as preference unless user wants strict traditional layout.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Cost estimation chat. Early cost bands use rate per sq.ft of built-up with
large uncertainty; final BOQ needs structural and finishes decisions.

===CHUNK===
Topic: School building basics. Institutional buildings need exits, assembly widths,
and fire separation per NBC — always specialist architects handle compliance.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Hospital planning. Hospitals need zoning of OPD, IPD, diagnostics, and
infection control — not a residential rule set.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Mall circulation. Malls need core placement, escalator banks, and fire refuge
floors per codes — retrieval should pull institutional chunks not house setbacks.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Apartment parking. One slot per unit is a common planning assumption; EV
charging and visitor parking are emerging add-ons.

===CHUNK===
Topic: Demo honesty. Prototype answers may simplify numbers; users must verify with
licensed professionals and official circulars.
