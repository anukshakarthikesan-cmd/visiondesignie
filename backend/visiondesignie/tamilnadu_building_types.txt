VISIONDESIGNIE — TAMIL NADU BUILDING TYPES (RAG)

INDEPENDENT HOUSES
- Ground / G+1 / G+2 common in Chennai suburbs on small and medium plots.
- Parking: stilt or front slot; watch ramp slope on narrow sites.

APARTMENTS
- Dominant in city cores and OMR belts; saleable vs carpet area confusion is frequent client question.
- Tall towers: fire stairs, lift, generator, STP — facility load on common area.

VILLAS
- Gated plots with controlled façade guidelines; often uniform roof or height lines.
- Families want garden, car count, and sometimes elevator even for G+2.

COMMERCIAL
- Ground retail, showrooms, clinics — parking and customer path first.
- IT parks and retail malls: specialist code — not house setback chat.

MIXED-USE
- Shop + residence stacked: separate meters, staircase access, and noise separation early in plan.
- Common in high-street Chennai parcels — approval class depends on use mix.
