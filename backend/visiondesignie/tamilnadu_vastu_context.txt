VISIONDESIGNIE — VASTU CONTEXT IN TAMIL NADU (RAG)

CULTURAL WEIGHT
- Many Tamil Nadu clients open design talks with vastu: kitchen sector, master bedroom, entry, pooja corner.
- Treat as strong preference layer alongside light, privacy, and structural grid.

COMMON DIRECTION TALK (CONVERSATION, NOT LEGAL)
- Kitchen: southeast is a frequent request in south Indian briefs.
- Master bedroom: southwest narrative appears often in traditional families.
- Pooja: northeast quiet zone is a common ask when plot allows.
- Main door: auspicious padam / direction preferences vary by family and advisor.

PRACTICAL MERGE
- Road noise, west sun, and neighbour windows may shift “ideal” sector slightly — architect explains trade-off.
- Toilets: clients often want them off kitchen sight lines and not “centre” of house — align with services stacking anyway.

CHATBOT TONE
- “We can respect your family’s vastu priorities where they fit ventilation and structure — final word is yours with your advisor.”
