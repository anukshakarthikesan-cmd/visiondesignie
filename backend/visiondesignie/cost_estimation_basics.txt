VISIONDESIGNIE — COST ESTIMATION BASICS (RAG)

COST PER SQ.FT IDEA
- Builders quote “per sq.ft” of built-up or saleable area — always ask which area they mean.
- Same city can vary 2× between economy spec and premium spec.

WHAT DRIVES COST
- Number of floors and foundation type (soft soil → higher).
- Finishes: flooring, kitchen, bath fittings, façade material.
- Complexity: large spans, cantilevers, basements, lifts.

MATERIAL COST FACTORS
- Cement, steel, sand rates move monthly — budget a contingency percent.
- Imported tiles and stone spike cost — local options often sufficient visually.

LABOR BASICS
- Skilled mason and bar bender availability affects speed and rate.
- Finishing stage labor spreads if design has many groove lines and levels.

BUDGET PLANNING IDEAS
- Phase 1: shell + basic services + rough floors.
- Phase 2: kitchen, wardrobes, paint, façade finish.
- Keep 10–15% contingency for unknowns on old plots or unclear soil.

WHAT CHATBOT SHOULD SAY
- Give bands and drivers, not fake precision — “verify with three local quotes.”
- Tie cost conversation to area, spec level, and timeline.
