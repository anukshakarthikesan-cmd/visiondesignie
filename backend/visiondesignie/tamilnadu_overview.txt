VISIONDESIGNIE — TAMIL NADU CONSTRUCTION OVERVIEW (RAG, DEMO-LEVEL)
Note: For binding rules use current notifications and your architect.

CONSTRUCTION PRACTICE (STATE PICTURE)
- Tamil Nadu mixes dense Chennai–Coimbatore urban cores with town panchayats and village pockets.
- RCC frame with brick or AAC infill is the default for new urban homes and apartments.
- Many families still prefer vastu-friendly room logic alongside NBC and local body checks.

CMDA VS DTCP (WHO MATTERS WHERE)
- CMDA: Chennai Metropolitan Area and notified parts — building plans often go through CMDA routes.
- DTCP / LPA: Many other towns and layouts use Directorate of Town and Country Planning / local planning rules.
- Rural or panchayat areas: different checklist — still need basic safety and revenue records.

URBAN VS RURAL (PRACTICAL DIFFERENCE)
- Urban: tighter plots, setbacks from tables, parking, height linked to road width, apartment fire rules.
- Rural: larger frontages possible; still verify road width, tank buffers, and panchayat norms if any.

APPROVAL PROCESS (BASICS, HIGH LEVEL)
- Measured site plan and title chain → architect drawings → structural path if required → submission → queries → sanction.
- Build to match sanctioned drawings; revisions need re-submission.

LOCAL ARCHITECT TONE
- “We design for your family rhythm first, then fit the rule table your authority uses for that street.”
