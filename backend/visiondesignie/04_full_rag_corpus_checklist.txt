VISIONDESIGNIE — FULL RAG CORPUS CHECKLIST & DATA SOURCES (INDIA)
Use this as a master list of what to ingest later. Tag each PDF with: region,
authority, year, and “superseded?” flag.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A — STATUTORY & REGULATORY (HIGH PRIORITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- National Building Code of India (latest published parts) — fire, life safety,
  accessibility, plumbing, electrical coordination.
- Local Development Control Regulations (DCR) for target cities:
  CMDA / DTCP Tamil Nadu, BBMP/BDA Karnataka, MCGM Maharashtra, GHMC Telangana,
  NDMC/SDMC Delhi variants, etc.
- Town and country planning acts summaries — only official PDFs or gazettes.
- Coastal regulation, airport funnel height, heritage precinct overlays where relevant.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B — PROFESSIONAL GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Council of Architecture (COA) public documents on practice and education.
- Architect’s Act context — what only registered architects may certify.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C — STRUCTURAL & SAFETY (REFERENCE, NOT DIY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- IS codes (earthquake wind loads) — store for RAG explanations; always defer final
  design to licensed structural engineer.
- Fire NOC pathways for high-rise and assembly occupancies — high-level process text.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D — SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- NBC service chapters summaries: water supply, drainage, rainwater harvesting.
- Basic electrical safety concepts: earthing, ELCB, generator norms — from standards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E — SUSTAINABILITY & GREEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ECBC / state energy codes summaries.
- IGBC/GRIHA overview documents — certification criteria at high level.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F — TYPOLOGY PACKS (USER QUERIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Residential: plotted housing, apartments 1–4 BHK, villas, row houses.
- Institutional: schools, colleges, hospitals, labs.
- Commercial: retail shops, malls, supermarkets, restaurants, hotels, banquet halls,
  theatres, banks, co-working.
- Industrial & warehouses: basic separation and fire notes only with official texts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G — PLANNING & DESIGN KNOWLEDGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Space planning: zoning, circulation, privacy, daylight, ventilation.
- Anthropometrics and furniture planning — standard textbooks (Ching, Neufert summaries
  you have rights to use).
- Materials and construction methods — lecture notes you own or open textbooks.
- Famous architects and case studies — short factual bios and project descriptions
  you are allowed to republish.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H — REGIONAL & SITE CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Climate zones of India — hot-dry, warm-humid, composite — orientation tips.
- Soil and contour: only generic education content unless you have site reports.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I — VASTU & CULTURAL (OPTIONAL LAYER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Treat as client preference layer; ingest only sources you have license to use.
- Never claim legal compliance from vastu chat alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
J — DATA TYPES TO COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Official PDF circulars, approved handbooks, sanctioned zoning maps (where open).
- Your own lecture notes, assignments, studio sheets (you own copyright).
- Licensed stock plan catalogs only if license allows embedding.
- Avoid pirated scans of paid books.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
K — WHERE TO LOOK (STARTING POINTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Government portals: TCP departments, urban local bodies, publication sections.
- BIS portal for standards catalog (purchase or library access).
- COA website for public notices.
- University open courseware (check license).
- Publisher sites for architecture textbooks (purchase digital where allowed).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L — METADATA TO STORE PER DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- title, authority, region, year, language, page_range, topic_tags[], reliability_tier
  (official / textbook / internal_note).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
M — CHUNKING STRATEGY FOR PRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Chunk by section headings in NBC/DCR; keep tables intact or duplicate headers.
- Store “effective date” in metadata for retrieval ranking.
