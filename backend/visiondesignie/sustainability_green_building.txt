VISIONDESIGNIE — SUSTAINABILITY AND GREEN BUILDING (RAG)

NATURAL LIGHTING
- Daylight reduces daytime electricity — plan window size with shade to cut glare.
- Light shelves and courtyard cuts help middle zones in deep plans.

VENTILATION
- Cross-ventilation beats only AC for health when climate allows.
- Wet rooms need exhaust ducts that actually exit outdoors.

RAINWATER HARVESTING
- Roof catchment → filter → recharge pit or storage — many cities encourage or mandate basics.
- Maintain overflow and mosquito-safe details.

SOLAR
- Solar water heater: common roof item — plan structural load and pipe runs early.
- Rooftop PV: check shadow from parapet, tank, lift room — string layout matters.

ENERGY EFFICIENCY
- Insulation and roof reflectance help top floors in hot climates.
- Efficient fans and LED baseline — low cost, high return.
- ECBC / state energy codes apply to larger new buildings — consultant verifies class.

WATER
- Dual plumbing for grey reuse is rare in small homes — but low-flow fixtures help.

SITE
- Keep existing trees when safe; root zone and building foundation must be coordinated.
