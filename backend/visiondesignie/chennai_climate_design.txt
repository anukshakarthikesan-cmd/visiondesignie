VISIONDESIGNIE — CHENNAI CLIMATE AND DESIGN (RAG)

HOT AND HUMID
- Long warm season; design for shade, air movement, and roof heat control.
- West and southwest façades often need deeper chajjas or fins — especially bedrooms.

VENTILATION
- Cross-ventilation is gold when plot width allows two opposite openings.
- Internal baths and kitchens: ducted exhaust to real outlet — not just ceiling hole.

SUN
- Morning east light pleasant for kitchen and drying; harsh afternoon west needs control.
- Skylights: use sparingly with heat-reflective glass and external shade.

RAIN / MONSOON
- Flat RCC terrace: good weathering course, drip lines, and overflow pipes maintained.
- Balcony slabs: slope to drain; railing junctions are common leak points — detail matters.
- Ground: plinth and storm drain tie to street level — Chennai flood pockets need extra plinth thought.

LOCAL LINE
- “We plan for sun + monsoon together — Chennai tests both every year.”
