VISIONDESIGNIE — DESIGN CONCEPTS (RAG)

MODERN VS TRADITIONAL
- Modern: clean lines, large glass with sun control, flat or subtle roof lines.
- Traditional: pitched roof, mouldings, verandah, column rhythm — maintenance plan upfront.

MINIMALIST
- Few materials, careful joints, hidden storage — needs discipline during construction.
- “Empty” look still needs good light and texture or it feels cold.

SPACE OPTIMIZATION
- Built-in furniture and straight corridors buy usable area in small plots.
- Double-height only where it earns drama — it steals upper floor area.

AESTHETIC BALANCE
- Rhythm: repeat window widths; one strong focal point (entry, feature wall).
- Color: 60% neutral base, 30% secondary, 10% accent — simple rule for harmony.

SCALE
- Human scale at entry — canopy or deep recess signals door; giant blank walls feel harsh.

CONTEXT
- Neighbourhood character: some areas prefer uniform parapet line; check local notes.

USER FEELING
- Ask: “Calm, grand, or playful?” — three different detail choices.
