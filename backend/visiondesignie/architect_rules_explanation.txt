VISIONDESIGNIE — RULES EXPLAINED SIMPLY (CMDA / NBC / PRACTICE) RAG

CMDA — WHAT IT MEANS TO A HOMEOWNER
- CMDA is the planning authority for Chennai metro limits — it checks setbacks, height, parking, and use.
- Your architect submits drawings; CMDA comments or approves per current rules.
- Different zones inside the city can have different controls — “same city, different rule strip.”

NBC — BIG PICTURE
- NBC is India’s National Building Code — minimum standards for safety and services.
- It guides stair width, fire exits in large buildings, accessibility ideas, and coordination between trades.
- Local bodies adopt NBC ideas in their own checklists.

HEIGHT LIMITS — HOW ARCHITECTS TALK ABOUT IT
- Height is not one number for all India — it ties to road width, plot size, and zone.
- A narrow lane may cap floors even if you “fit” in FSI on paper.

PARKING — TYPICAL EXPECTATION
- Many norms expect car parking for houses above a size threshold; stall size is standardized in practice.
- Apartments often plan one bay per unit plus visitor slots depending on size class.

FIRE & LIFE SAFETY — WHEN IT MATTERS MORE
- Small independent houses: basic exit and stove safety still matter.
- Tall apartments and public buildings: fire stairs, refuge, sprinkler rules escalate — NBC is strict there.

SETBACK TABLES — HOW TO READ THEM
- Tables list minimum meters by plot width or road class — your site drawing must show those lines.
- Corner plots may add splay or extra margin on second frontage.

PWD / LOCAL ROADS
- Entry must not block storm flow; apron and drain details tie to road department conditions sometimes.

APPROVAL SEQUENCE — SIMPLE ORDER
- Survey → concept plan → structural scheme → services → submission → queries → sanction.
- Changes after sanction need revision drawings.
