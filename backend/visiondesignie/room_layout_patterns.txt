VISIONDESIGNIE — ROOM LAYOUT PATTERNS (RAG)

1 BHK PATTERN
- Entry → short passage or direct living.
- Kitchen: linear or L along one wall; dining two-seater in living edge if tight.
- One bedroom: bed on solid wall, wardrobe on side wall, one window for air.
- One bath: common, near bedroom/living junction for night use.

2 BHK PATTERN
- Living-dining spine; kitchen adjacent to dining.
- Two bedrooms: one near front for guest flexibility, one quieter at rear if noise map fits.
- Baths: one common + one attached if budget allows; else one large split wet-dry.

3 BHK PATTERN
- Master with bath; two kids/guest rooms share bath or one extra bath.
- Utility and store near kitchen; balcony off living or master if façade allows.

FURNITURE BASICS
- Sofa length needs clear wall; TV wall opposite with no glare window behind screen.
- Dining: 4-seat needs ~3 ft per chair side; allow pull-out space.
- Bed: 6 ft mattress + 2 ft circulation at foot; wardrobe 2 ft depth clear inside room.

KITCHEN COUNTERS
- Sink → fridge → stove triangle; minimum counter for prep on at least one side.

STUDY NOOK
- 3–4 ft desk width by window; plug points at desk height planned early.
