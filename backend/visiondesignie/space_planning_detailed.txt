VISIONDESIGNIE — SPACE PLANNING (DETAILED, RAG)

ZONING
- Public zone: entry, living, guest-facing areas.
- Private zone: bedrooms, family TV nook, study behind a visual buffer.
- Service zone: kitchen, utility, store, toilets — short paths, easy maintenance.

CIRCULATION FLOW
- Straight passages waste less area than zig-zag in small homes.
- Avoid bedroom doors opening directly opposite main toilet door when possible.
- Guest path should not cut through private corridor if avoidable.

ROOM ADJACENCY
- Kitchen next to dining (short carry distance).
- Common toilet reachable from living without walking through bedrooms.
- Dirty linen / wash near drying yard or utility when possible.

FUNCTIONAL PRINCIPLES
- Morning kitchen sun is pleasant in many climates; harsh west sun hits bedrooms harder — tune by city.
- Storage near kitchen and bedrooms — not only “leftover corners.”
- Stair core: side stack frees rectangular room bays; center stack splits plan — pick by frontage depth.

VENTILATION LOGIC
- Cross-ventilation: openings on two walls beats single wall when plot allows.
- Internal rooms: borrowed light via duct, skylight, or high window — plan early.

LIGHTING LOGIC
- Living and kitchen benefit from good daylight; reduce glare with sill depth and overhang.
- Bedroom: controllable blinds better than one huge west glass without shade.

NOISE
- Place quieter rooms away from road side if traffic is heavy.
- Service yard and pump noise — keep away from master bedroom wall when possible.
