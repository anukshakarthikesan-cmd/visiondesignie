VISIONDESIGNIE — CHENNAI LOCALITY INSIGHTS (PRACTICE NOTES, RAG)
General planning tone — not valuation or legal zoning replacement.

ANNA NAGAR
- Mature grid blocks; mix of older independent houses and mid-rise apartments.
- Typical plots: many legacy 2400 / 1200 sq.ft equivalents in older layouts; newer pockets tighter.
- Demand: upgrade builds, flat sales, school proximity premium.

VELACHERY
- Dense residential plus retail edges; metro access changed daily patterns.
- Plot sizes: varied — small frontages near main roads; deeper plots inward.
- Demand: 2–3 BHK apartments, rental stock; traffic noise drives façade and bedroom placement.

TAMBARAM / PERIPHERY
- Mix of CMDA-notified and expanding suburban belts — always confirm which authority.
- Often 600–2000 sq.ft plotted layouts; individual houses and small apartments.
- Demand: affordable ground-plus-floor builds; parking and water table talk matters.

OMR (IT CORRIDOR)
- High-rise apartments, gated communities; office strips.
- Less “empty plot” chat, more flat carpet vs saleable area and amenities.
- Demand: young professionals; work-from-home corners inside 2–3 BHK.

PORUR / WEST CHENNAI POCKETS
- Mix of villas, row houses, and apartments near lakes and tech parks.
- Plot: gated villa lots to medium plots in older layouts.
- Demand: family homes; check buffer language if near water body.

T. NAGAR / CORE COMMERCIAL
- Retail ground floors, tight sites, assembly and fire logic for crowded buildings.
- Residential is often older apartments or upper floors — not greenfield house plots.
- Demand: commercial renovation, façade upgrades; parking is painful — realistic expectations.
