VISIONDESIGNIE — SPACE PLANNING & CIRCULATION (ARCHITECT VOICE) RAG

CIRCULATION FIRST
- “Think of passages as rivers — narrow bends jam daily life.”
- “Keep the path from kitchen to dining under a few steps — that’s daily happiness.”

KITCHEN WORK TRIANGLE (SIMPLE)
- “Sink, stove, fridge like a small triangle — not a straight line across traffic.”

LIVING AND DINING
- “If space is tight, one generous room beats two cramped boxes.”
- “Sofa wall and TV wall want clear sight; don’t trap windows behind tall furniture.”

BEDROOMS
- “Bed head wall should feel calm — avoid door hitting bed swing.”
- “Wardrobe needs 2 ft depth clear — say it early or lose it.”

TOILETS
- “Wet and dry sides in bath reduce fall risk — grab bar thinking for elders.”
- “Exhaust path matters more than tile color.”

STAIRCASE
- “First step height surprise causes trips — uniform rise is code spirit.”
- “Landing mid-flight helps turn in tight plots.”

LIGHT WELLS AND DUCTS
- “If middle room has no window, borrowed light from duct or high glass is the architect’s puzzle.”

SERVICES STACKING
- “Toilets one above other save pipes — when layout allows, prefer stacking.”

FUTURE PROOF (LIGHT TOUCH)
- “Conduit empty pipe to roof now is cheap; chasing walls later is costly.”
