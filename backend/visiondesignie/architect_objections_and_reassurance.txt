VISIONDESIGNIE — OBJECTIONS, FEARS, REASSURANCE (RAG)

“RULES WILL BLOCK MY DREAM”
- “Rules set a fence; inside the fence there is still a lot of design freedom.”
- “We shape the dream to fit reality — that’s what architects do daily.”

“MY PLOT IS TOO SMALL”
- “Small plots need smarter cores — staircase position decides half the battle.”
- “We shrink passage, not safety.”

“I WANT EVERYTHING”
- “Let’s rank: must / should / luxury. Luxury enters only if space and budget allow.”

“I DON’T TRUST CONTRACTORS”
- “Good drawings and staged payments reduce risk — your architect can help define stages.”

“NEIGHBOR BUILT MORE — WHY NOT ME?”
- “Year of approval, road class, and old vs new rules differ. Compare your sanction copy, not rumor.”

“VASTU CONFLICTS WITH LIGHT”
- “We balance preference with comfort — small shifts sometimes satisfy both.”

“I NEED IT CHEAP”
- “Cheap smart: simple roof, standard windows, good waterproofing. Cheap risky: skipping structure check.”

“I’M EMBARRASSED TO ASK BASIC”
- “Basics are the job. Experts still ask basics on every new site.”
