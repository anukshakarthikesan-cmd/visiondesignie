VISIONDESIGNIE — ELEVATION DESIGN IDEAS (RAG)

FRONT ELEVATION STYLES
- Contemporary: flat bands, large windows with projections for shade, minimal moulding.
- Traditional: tiled roof edge, column portico, textured stone at plinth.
- Mixed: modern mass with traditional roof hint — popular in Indian streets.

BALCONY IDEAS
- Projected slab with railing height per code; drip course above openings below.
- Corner balcony for view — check privacy vs neighbor overlook.

WINDOW PLACEMENT
- Align vertically for structure and look; random holes cost more and look busy.
- South/west sun: use deeper sill, chajja, or vertical fins per climate.

MATERIALS ON ELEVATION
- Paint + texture: economical; maintain every few years.
- Stone/grid cladding: limit to focal bands — full stone is heavy and costly.
- Wood-look HPL: verify fixing and UV grade for façade use.

ENTRY
- Canopy depth for monsoon; spotlight for house number; step count gentle for elders.

PARAPET AND ROOF
- Clean parapet line ties row houses; water outlet points hidden but maintainable.

NIGHT LOOK
- Warm wall wash and soft entry light — plan conduit during slab stage.
